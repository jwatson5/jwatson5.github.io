About these recordings

  • These are filtered and level-adjusted listening copies for transcription assistance. They are not the original recordings or certified transcripts.
  • Microphone labels identify channels, not verified speakers. A voice may be picked up by several microphones; individual tracks may include room noise.
  • The files retain the full extracted recording timeline; output durations match the extracted source tracks. Use elapsed recording time when switching between the mix and individual tracks. This is not the time of day or a transcript page/line reference.
  • The supplied recordings may include breaks and other cases. They have not been cut to Watson-only sections, and no case start/end markers have been added.
  • Each hearing includes a stereo listening mix and five standard mono tracks: Judge, Witness, Plaintiff, Defense, and Clerk. October 12, 2026 also includes separately cleaned Mic 6, Mic 7, and Webex tracks. Those three additional channels are not included in its main mix.
  • All files are FLAC listening copies at 16 kHz / 16-bit. This page contains the two equitable distribution hearings.
